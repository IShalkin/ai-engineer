# Fraud, Adverse Action, and Model Risk Guardrails

Engineering controls for AI that detects fraud or financial crime, or that takes any decision going against a person. These controls help an accountable owner discharge an obligation that owner already holds; nothing here is legal advice and the obligation never transfers to engineering.

Instruments named are US federal banking and consumer-credit sources unless marked otherwise. Regulation is jurisdiction- and version-specific: confirm the instrument, edition, and amendment in force, and confirm the guidance has not been withdrawn, before designing to any clause in it.

## FRD-01 - Model Risk Governance

The 2011 interagency `Supervisory Guidance on Model Risk Management` (Federal Reserve SR 11-7; OCC Bulletin 2011-12; adopted by FDIC as FIL-22-2017) defines a model as "a quantitative method, system, or approach that applies statistical, economic, financial, or mathematical theories, techniques, and assumptions to process input data into quantitative estimates", consisting of "an information input component", "a processing component", and "a reporting component". The definition "also covers quantitative approaches whose inputs are partially or wholly qualitative or based on expert judgment, provided that the output is quantitative in nature". `CURRENT_PRIMARY`

1. **An LLM does not escape the frame by being a prompt.** A prompted model that emits a score, rank, tier, probability, or threshold comparison meets the definition on its plain terms: prompt and weights are the processing component, retrieved context is input, the emitted score is reporting. The same guidance adds that even approaches outside the definition "should also be subject to a rigorous control process". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` There is no configuration of an LLM that is both decision-bearing and ungoverned.
2. **Tier by risk, not by technology.** The guidance states model risk "increases with greater model complexity, higher uncertainty about inputs and assumptions, broader use, and larger potential impact", that materiality governs how extensive the framework must be, and that rigor of validation "should be commensurate with" model complexity and materiality. `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Tier on decision reversibility, subject harm, volume, and autonomy; record the tier and its rationale as an inventory attribute.
3. **Inventory.** Maintain a firm-wide inventory covering models in use, under development, and recently retired, recording: purpose and products; actual or expected usage and any restrictions on use; type and source of inputs; underlying components including other models; outputs and their intended use; whether the model is functioning properly; when last updated; policy exceptions; named individuals responsible for each aspect; and the time frame during which the model is expected to remain valid. Any variation warranting separate validation is a separate cross-referenced entry. `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Prompts, tool definitions, retrieval indexes, feature sets, and thresholds are versioned inventory attributes, not untracked config. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS`
4. **Three parts.** Development/implementation/use; validation; governance, policies, and controls. The guiding principle is "effective challenge" - "critical analysis by objective, informed parties who can identify model limitations and assumptions and produce appropriate changes" - which "depends on a combination of incentives, competence, and influence". `CURRENT_PRIMARY`

| Validation element | Verified content | Artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Conceptual soundness, including developmental evidence | design and construction quality, assumptions, variable choice, data relevance, "comparison with alternative theories and approaches", sensitivity analysis | design record, baseline ladder, sensitivity report |
| Ongoing monitoring, including process verification and benchmarking | input accuracy and completeness, code change control, comparison of inputs and outputs "to estimates from alternative internal or external data or models", override analysis | monitoring plan, benchmark comparison, override ledger |
| Outcomes analysis, including back-testing | outputs against realized outcomes; back-testing over "a sample time period not used in model development and at an observation frequency that matches the forecast horizon or performance window" | back-test record, champion-challenger result |

5. **Independence with teeth.** Validation should be done by people not responsible for development or use and with no stake in the verdict; where developers do part of it, an independent party must critically review and conduct additional activities. Independence "should be judged by actions and outcomes". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` The evidence is a list of models actually changed because validation said so. A validation function that has never blocked a release has not been evidenced.
6. **Simple baseline is a required comparator, not a courtesy.** Benchmarking is defined as comparison to alternative internal or external data or models, usable in development and in ongoing monitoring. `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Carry a constant, a rule, and a small transparent model through every release as live comparators.
7. **Overrides are a defect signal.** Track override reasons and override performance: "if the rate of overrides is high, or if the override process consistently improves model performance, it is often a sign that the underlying model needs revision or redevelopment". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Do not report a high override rate as evidence that human review is working.
8. **Documentation standard.** Documentation of development and validation must be "sufficiently detailed so that parties unfamiliar with a model can understand how the model operates, its limitations, and its key assumptions". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` That is the acceptance criterion for a model card, and it implies a third party can reconstruct a specific decision from the record.
9. **Cadence and ownership.** Conduct a periodic review "at least annually but more frequently if warranted"; material changes are validated before implementation. `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Every inventory entry names a development owner, a validation owner, and a business owner who carries the loss.
10. **Vendor and third-party models.** Validate your own use: require developmental evidence, test results, stated limitations and where use may be problematic; monitor outcomes on your own data; hold a contingency plan for the vendor becoming unavailable. `CURRENT_PRIMARY` The 2021 interagency BSA/AML statement adds that an appropriate level of validation independence "is also important when banks outsource multiple functions to the same third party" - a direct constraint on buying model, monitoring, and validation from one supplier. `CURRENT_PRIMARY`

Supervisory guidance of this class "does not have the force and effect of law" (stated of the model risk guidance in the 2021 interagency statement). `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Treat it as the expected control standard, not as the source of the underlying legal duty.

## FRD-02 - Detection System Design

1. **Accuracy is not a metric here.** At fraud and financial-crime base rates a constant negative prediction scores near-perfect and detects nothing. Report precision, recall, and PR-AUC at the deployed operating point, plus detection at fixed review capacity and alerts per investigator-hour. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS`
2. **Cost-weight the errors.** Fraud loss, investigation cost, harm to a wrongly frozen customer, and regulatory consequence differ by orders of magnitude. Select the operating point on the cost-weighted curve and name the owner of that cost function. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS`
3. **Alert volume is a capacity constraint, not an optimisation target.** The 2021 interagency BSA/AML statement recognises that objectives of most such models "place greater emphasis on coverage over efficiency", and that a bank "may choose to accept a reduction in efficiency (such as by producing more alerts) in exchange for greater coverage". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` A queue-length SLO must never be allowed to retune coverage down silently; make any coverage change an explicit approved decision.
4. **Label latency and a self-poisoning feedback loop.** Blocked activity never reveals ground truth and unalerted activity is unlabelled by construction, so training on dispositions alone teaches the model its own past policy. Version every label with disposition, decision timestamp, and the model version that produced the decision, and keep a randomised or below-threshold sample outside the policy to estimate the missed population. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS` The 2021 statement recognises testing may differ from other models because of "the lack of information about realized outcomes (e.g., Suspicious Activity Reports)". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Where no outcome label exists, substitute benchmarking, coverage testing, and typology scenario sets, and say so in the validation report instead of passing a proxy off as an outcome.
5. **Threshold testing on both sides.** Sample alerts at or above the threshold, and sample activity suppressed below it, to show the suppressed population is genuinely low-risk; retain both samples as evidence. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS` This is the engineering form of the examination expectation that management "periodically review and test the filtering criteria and thresholds established to ensure that they are still effective" and that "the monitoring system's programming methodology and effectiveness should be independently validated to ensure that the models are detecting potentially suspicious activity" (FFIEC BSA/AML Examination Manual, suspicious activity reporting section). `CURRENT_PRIMARY`
6. **Who may move a threshold.** The same manual states "the authority to establish or change expected activity profiles should be clearly defined through policies and procedures", that "controls should ensure limited access to the monitoring systems, and changes should generally require the approval of the BSA compliance officer or senior management", and that management "should document and be able to explain filtering criteria, thresholds used, and how both are appropriate for the bank's risks". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Required change record: prior value, new value, alert-volume and detection impact, both-side sample evidence, named approver, effective date, rollback value. A threshold in a mutable config file with no approver is an ungoverned model change.
7. **Champion-challenger is outcomes analysis, not a preference poll.** Parallel outcomes analysis compares the adjusted model against the original on realized outcomes; "if the adjusted model does not outperform the original model", additional change "or even a wholesale redesign" is likely needed before replacement. `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Shadow the candidate on live traffic with no effect, compare on identical cases, then canary a bounded population carrying the offline metrics through, retaining a rollback version of model, thresholds, and features together. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS`
8. **Drift here is adversarial, not incidental.** The opponent is actively probing the deployed control, so distribution shift is partly a security signal. Monitor input and score distributions, alert mix by typology, precision on newly labelled cases, and cohort performance; alert on outcome degradation, not only pipeline health. Rate-limit and log score-exposing surfaces, do not return granular scores to untrusted callers, and rotate enumerable elements. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS` The 2021 statement recognises such models "may require quick adjustments to reflect the changing nature of criminal behavior". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Pre-approve which change classes take the fast path and what evidence each needs; do not invent governance mid-incident.
9. **Pilots are protected, not exempt.** The 2018 `Joint Statement on Innovative Efforts to Combat Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing` (Federal Reserve, FDIC, FinCEN, NCUA, OCC) states that pilot programs "in and of themselves should not subject banks to supervisory criticism even if the pilot programs ultimately prove unsuccessful", that pilots exposing gaps "will not necessarily result in supervisory action", and that where an AI-based monitoring system identifies suspicious activity existing processes would have missed, the agencies "will not automatically assume that the banks' existing processes are deficient". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Detecting more is not self-incrimination. Run pilots alongside the production control, not instead of it.

## FRD-03 - Adverse Action and Explainability

**Hard rule.** An automated or agentic system must not take or recommend an adverse action against a person unless four things exist at decision time: a specific reason that can be stated to that person, an evidence record, human reviewability, and an appeal path. Absent any one, the system proposes and a named human decides.

1. **Reasons must be specific and must be the real ones.** Under Regulation B the statement of reasons for adverse action "must be specific and indicate the principal reason(s) for the adverse action", and "Statements that the adverse action was based on the creditor's internal standards or policies or that the applicant, joint applicant, or similar party failed to achieve a qualifying score on the creditor's credit scoring system are insufficient" (12 CFR 1002.9(b)(2)); the notice is generally due within 30 days of a completed application (12 CFR 1002.9(a)(1)(i)) and contains either a statement of specific reasons or a disclosure of the right to obtain one (12 CFR 1002.9(a)(2)). `CURRENT_PRIMARY`
2. **Derived from the decision, not narrated after it.** Official commentary requires that the specific reasons disclosed "must relate to and accurately describe the factors actually considered or scored by a creditor" (12 CFR part 1002, Supp. I, comment 9(b)(2)-2), and for a scoring system that "the reasons disclosed must relate only to those factors actually scored in the system" and "no factor that was a principal reason for adverse action may be excluded from disclosure" (comment 9(b)(2)-4). More than four reasons "is not likely to be helpful to the applicant" (comment 9(b)(2)-1). `CURRENT_PRIMARY`
3. **A generated explanation that does not causally reflect the decision is a defect.** Reasons must be bound to the deployed model version, that subject's scored feature values, and the attributions actually produced by the scoring path. An LLM may render an accurate reason set into readable language; it may not select, rank, or invent reasons. Constrain it to a validated code set and diff its output against the source attributions before disclosure. A narrative produced by a model that never saw the attributions is fabrication with good grammar, and post-hoc surrogate explanations are approximations whose fidelity must itself be measured and reported. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS`
4. **Reason capability is a release gate.** If the architecture cannot emit accurate principal reasons, it is not shippable for that decision. Reason generation cannot be added after launch, because the attribution evidence has to be captured at decision time. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS`
5. **Parallel disclosure regimes do not substitute for one another.** Commentary states that "Disclosing the key factors that adversely affected the consumer's credit score does not satisfy the ECOA requirement to disclose specific reasons" (comment 9(b)(2)-9), while FCRA separately requires disclosure of the "key factors that adversely affected the credit score of the consumer", defined as all relevant elements listed in order of importance and generally capped at four (15 U.S.C. 1681g(f)(1)(C), 1681g(f)(2)(B), 1681m(a)(2)). `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Emit both from the same evidence record; neither discharges the other.
6. **Fair-treatment testing at a controls level.** The disparate-impact theory under this specific regulation is in motion, so do not anchor the control to it. Regulation B previously carried official commentary explaining an "effects test" for facially neutral creditor practices; a 2026 CFPB final rule (91 FR 21620, effective July 21, 2026) deleted that commentary and revised 12 CFR 1002.6(a) to state that the Act "does not provide that the 'effects test' applies for determining whether there is discrimination in violation of the Act". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Cohort testing survives that change on engineering grounds and because other statutes, other regulators, and other jurisdictions are untouched by it; the disclosure duties in FRD-03.1 to FRD-03.5 are also unaffected. The revalidation duty is still codified: a creditor "is responsible for ensuring its system is validated and revalidated based on the creditor's own data when it becomes available" (12 CFR part 1002, Supp. I, comment 2(p)-3). `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Controls: test outcome and error rates by cohort before release and on a schedule after; test features and candidate proxies, not only the final decision; and for each retained feature record the stated justification and the alternatives actually evaluated, keeping that search reviewable. Which legal theory the testing serves is the accountable owner's call, not engineering's.
7. **Financial-crime decisions carry their own documentation record.** The FFIEC BSA/AML manual states banks "should document SAR decisions, including the specific reason for filing or not filing a SAR", that a named decision maker should hold authority to make the final filing decision under a clearly defined escalation process, and that examiners "should focus on whether the bank has an effective SAR decision-making process, not individual SAR decisions". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` A SAR is filed no later than 30 calendar days from initial detection, extended to 60 if no suspect can be identified (31 CFR 1020.320(b)(3)); a copy and supporting documentation are retained five years from filing (31 CFR 1020.320(d)); and a SAR and any information revealing its existence are confidential (31 CFR 1020.320(e)). `CURRENT_PRIMARY`
8. **Appeal is a data path, not a mailbox.** A route to human reconsideration must exist, be disclosed where required, and feed its outcomes back as labels under FRD-02.4.

Interpretive guidance in this area moves, and a module of this kind ages against it. The two CFPB circulars on adverse action and complex algorithms (Circular 2022-03 and Circular 2023-03) were withdrawn by the CFPB notice `Interpretive Rules, Policy Statements, and Advisory Opinions; Withdrawal` (90 FR 20084), withdrawals applicable as of May 12, 2025. `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Do not cite them as current authority. The disclosure requirements cited in FRD-03.1 to FRD-03.5 were read in the current codified text and are unaffected by that withdrawal; the effects-test commentary discussed in FRD-03.6 was separately deleted by rulemaking. In the EU, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 Article 86 gives an affected person subject to a decision taken by a deployer on the basis of output from a high-risk AI system listed in Annex III (excepting point 2) that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects them the right to obtain from the deployer "clear and meaningful explanations of the role of the AI system in the decision-making procedure and the main elements of the decision taken", subject to the carve-outs in its paragraphs 2 and 3. `CURRENT_PRIMARY` That right binds the deployer, so an engineering record adequate for FRD-03.3 is a precondition for it.

## FRD-04 - Agent-Specific Fraud Controls

What changes when the system acts rather than scores: the second cause of model risk becomes dominant. The guidance names it directly - even a sound model producing accurate outputs "may exhibit high model risk if it is misapplied or misused", and this "is even more of a concern if a model is used outside the environment for which it was designed". `CURRENT_PRIMARY` An agent that reuses a fraud score for a purpose that score was never validated for is that failure, executing at machine rate.

1. **Authority is enforced outside the model.** Every consequential capability is a typed tool with an allowlisted destination, an authenticated actor, a tenant and subject binding, and a policy decision taken by a policy engine the model cannot address. Prompt text is not a permission. Bind actor, subject, objective, target resource, operation, preconditions, policy verdict, approval where required, idempotency key, and expiry before the effect; observe the destination independently afterwards and record the actual outcome. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS`
2. **Per-action and per-window budgets.** Cap actions per subject, per agent, per tool, and per time window, with a distinct cap on aggregate customer impact (accounts frozen per hour, payments blocked per hour). Budget exhaustion is a terminal state that escalates to a human, never a retry loop.
3. **Irreversible-action gate.** Classify each action reversible, compensatable, or irreversible. Irreversible or externally visible actions against a person require preview and human approval showing the exact intended action, destination, evidence, and uncertainty, with the proposal and the decision both persisted. Account closure, customer exit, and regulatory filings sit here by default.
4. **Idempotency and compensation for freezes.** A freeze must carry a caller-supplied idempotency key so a retry cannot double-apply, and a defined compensating action (unfreeze plus notification plus reversal of downstream declines) that is tested, not assumed. Reconcile agent-believed state against system-of-record state on a schedule; divergence is an incident.
5. **Prompt injection is a fraud vector, not only a security one.** The moment an agent reads attacker-supplied content - a merchant description, dispute narrative, uploaded document, chat message, callback payload - the adversary has a channel into the decision loop and a direct financial motive to use it. Treat all such content as untrusted data that cannot redefine policy or authority; separate instructions from content structurally; keep the surfaces that read attacker content away from the surfaces that hold authority, and require approval where they must meet. Pattern filters are telemetry, not a boundary. Test direct, indirect, and stored injection, including poisoned memory recalled in a later session. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS`
6. **Coordination and correlated failure across agents.** Multiple agents sharing a model, prompt, feature store, or retrieval index are not independent evidence; agreement among them is correlated, and a single poisoned artifact moves all of them together. Do not treat multi-agent concurrence as corroboration for an adverse action, and never let two agents' outputs jointly clear a gate that a single agent could not clear alone. Fence concurrent workers so two agents cannot act on the same subject at once. `ENGINEERING_SYNTHESIS`
7. **Statutory clocks are deterministic timers.** Filing and notification deadlines under FRD-03.7 and FRD-03.1 are implemented as scheduled escalations outside the agent loop, with alerting before breach. An agent may prepare and recommend; it may not own a deadline.
8. **Confidentiality partitioning.** Because SAR existence is confidential (31 CFR 1020.320(e)), SAR-derived context lives in a segregated store excluded from any customer-facing retrieval path, and no agent output to a subject may confirm, hint at, or explain such a filing. `CURRENT_PRIMARY` Enforce by index partition and destination allowlist, not by instruction.
